Settings

Changing a setting stops the metronome and starts the wall fresh.

Click on beats

Default is all four beats. For the jazz hi-hat feel, turn off 1 and 3.

Count in

Count-in clicks every beat, and brick 1 lands right as it ends. Off: brick 1 drops the moment you press play.

Bricks per wall

Each wall is one chorus: 16 or 32 bars.

Visual offset 0 ms
